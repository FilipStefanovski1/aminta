# Scoring Rubric

Nine dimensions, each scored **1–10 independently** — never averaged into
a single number without also reporting the breakdown (a 9/10 on
naturalness and a 2/10 on relevance is a different failure than two 5.5s,
and averaging erases that). Each score must come with one sentence of
justification pointing at something specific in the actual output, not a
general impression.

Score bands below are anchors, not hard boundaries — use judgment, but if
a score can't be justified against the band's description, the band is
probably wrong.

## 1. Relevance
Does it actually address the given topic/source post/draft?

- **1–2**: Ignores the input; generic content that could follow any prompt.
- **3–4**: Loosely related but misses the specific point of the input.
- **5–6**: On-topic but generic — could apply to several similar inputs, not clearly *this* one.
- **7–8**: Directly engages the specific detail/claim/number in the input.
- **9–10**: Sharp, unmistakably tied to the exact input — couldn't be reused for a similar-but-different input.

## 2. Originality
Does it avoid generic phrasing and predictable structure?

- **1–2**: Could have been generated by any generic AI writing tool for any topic.
- **3–4**: Mostly generic with one specific detail bolted on.
- **5–6**: Competent but formulaic — recognizable "AI post" shape (setup → insight → close).
- **7–8**: Has a genuine angle or specific observation, not just a rephrase of the input.
- **9–10**: Feels like it required actually thinking about this specific thing, not pattern-matching a genre.

## 3. Naturalness
Does the prose read like something a person actually wrote, sentence by sentence?

- **1–2**: Stilted, translated-sounding, or robotic phrase-by-phrase.
- **3–4**: Grammatically fine but rhythm/word choice reads as artificial.
- **5–6**: Reads fine but slightly "composed" — nothing wrong, nothing alive.
- **7–8**: Reads like natural writing; only minor tells.
- **9–10**: Indistinguishable from a real person typing quickly.

## 4. Conversational quality (reply-specific — score N/A for post/polish/template unless it's a reply)
Does it function as a real contribution to a conversation, not a monologue?

- **1–2**: A standalone statement that ignores the fact it's a reply.
- **3–4**: Acknowledges the post but adds nothing (pure agreement/restatement).
- **5–6**: Responds to the post but generically (could reply to many similar posts).
- **7–8**: Responds to the *specific* claim/detail with a real reaction.
- **9–10**: Reads like an actual reply a specific, engaged person would leave — advances the exchange.

## 5. Style consistency
Does it match the active persona's actual traits (from `personas.md`'s examples), not a caricature of them?

- **1–2**: Contradicts the persona (e.g. persona A's terse/lowercase voice producing a formal capitalized paragraph).
- **3–4**: Vaguely in the right register but misses specific traits (punctuation, rhythm, directness).
- **5–6**: Matches surface traits (casing, rough tone) but not rhythm/vocabulary/humor style.
- **7–8**: Matches most traits convincingly.
- **9–10**: Reads as if the persona's own examples wrote it — without tipping into caricature (check specifically: is it EXAGGERATING the trait beyond what the examples showed?).

## 6. Readability
Is it easy to read start to finish without re-reading?

- **1–2**: Confusing, run-on, or structurally broken.
- **3–4**: Readable but clunky — awkward clause ordering, unclear referents.
- **5–6**: Clear but flat.
- **7–8**: Clean, easy flow.
- **9–10**: Effortless — the structure disappears and only the content registers.

## 7. Formatting
Line breaks, punctuation, capitalization, absence of labels/quotes/markdown — does it look like a real, postable X post/reply?

- **1–2**: Contains a label ("Tweet:"), wrapping quotes, markdown, or broken punctuation.
- **3–4**: Clean of labels/quotes but formatting doesn't fit the length (e.g. forced line breaks in a one-liner).
- **5–6**: Acceptable but generic paragraph shape regardless of content.
- **7–8**: Formatting matches what the content actually needs.
- **9–10**: Formatting is invisible — exactly as much structure as the idea needs, no more.

## 8. Usefulness
Would this, if posted, actually accomplish what the user asked for (inform, entertain, advance a conversation, announce something)?

- **1–2**: Accomplishes nothing — filler.
- **3–4**: Technically on-topic but wouldn't land with a real audience.
- **5–6**: Serviceable — would read as a normal, unremarkable post.
- **7–8**: Would genuinely read well in a real timeline.
- **9–10**: Would stand out — the kind of post/reply that gets engagement because it's actually good.

## 9. Likelihood a real human would post it unchanged
The bottom-line score — synthesizes the above, but is not their average. Answer: would *you* hit "Insert" and post this without editing a single word?

- **1–2**: No — obviously needs a rewrite.
- **3–4**: No — needs meaningful edits (a phrase, the ending, the length).
- **5–6**: Maybe — would tweak one or two words but the structure's fine.
- **7–8**: Yes, with maybe a single small edit.
- **9–10**: Yes, exactly as generated.
